# Privacy Policy for Patchwork Farm

**Effective Date:** August 21, 2026

nnwh ("we," "us," or "our") built the Patchwork Farm app as a free app. This
SERVICE is provided by nnwh at no cost and is intended for use as is.

Patchwork Farm is a cozy planting puzzle. The game itself runs entirely on your
device: your settings and best times never leave it, and **we do not collect,
store, or share your personal information**. The app does show advertisements,
and our advertising partner (Google AdMob) does collect some device information
in order to serve them. Section 3 explains exactly what that involves.

This page is used to inform visitors regarding our policies with the collection,
use, and disclosure of Personal Information if anyone decided to use our
Service. If you choose to use our Service, then you agree to the practices
described in this Privacy Policy.

## 1. Information Collection and Use

We do **not** collect any personally identifiable information through Patchwork
Farm. The app does not require an account, does not ask you to sign in, and does
not transmit your game data to us or to any server of ours.

Patchwork Farm stores a small amount of information **locally on your device
only**, so the game can remember your preferences and progress:

- Your settings (show timer, gentle haptics, and gentle sounds);
- Your best completion times for each difficulty;
- Small counters recording how many fields you have planted and how many times
  the app has been opened, used to decide when to show the app store's native
  "rate this app" prompt and when an advertisement is due.

This information never leaves your device, is not accessible to us, and is
permanently removed when you uninstall the app.

Separately from the above, our advertising provider collects device information
directly from your device in order to serve ads. We never see that information
in a form that identifies you. See Section 3.

## 2. Third-Party Services

Patchwork Farm uses the following third-party services:

- **Google AdMob** — serves the advertisements described in Section 3. AdMob
  collects device information to select and measure ads.
- **App store review prompt** — the app may ask you to rate it using the native
  review feature provided by Google Play or the Apple App Store. This dialog is
  handled entirely by the platform; we do not receive your review or any
  personal data from it.
- **Fonts and artwork** — bundled inside the app and loaded locally, with no
  network request.

## 3. Advertising

Patchwork Farm displays advertisements supplied by **Google AdMob**.

**Where ads appear.** Ads are shown as an occasional full-screen
("interstitial") ad, and only at one point: after you have finished planting a
field and chosen to start another. Ads never appear while you are working a
field, never cover the board, and are deliberately infrequent — the app enforces
a minimum gap between them, does not show any ad during your first session, and
does not show any until you have completed several fields.

**What is collected.** To select and measure ads, Google AdMob may collect and
process:

- Your device's **advertising identifier** (the Android Advertising ID, or on
  Apple devices the Identifier for Advertisers where you have permitted it);
- **Device and technical information**, such as device model, operating system
  version, language, coarse location derived from your IP address, and your IP
  address itself;
- **Ad interaction data**, such as which ads were shown, whether an ad was
  viewed or clicked, and crash or performance diagnostics relating to the ad.

This information is collected by Google directly from your device. We do not
receive it, and we cannot link it to you. We receive only aggregate, anonymous
reporting — for example, how many ads were shown in total.

**Your choices.** You can limit or reset ad personalization at any time using
the controls built into your device:

- **Android:** Settings → Google → All services → Ads, where you can delete or
  reset your advertising ID and opt out of ad personalization.
- **iOS:** Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, where you can refuse app
  tracking; and Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising.

Opting out does not remove ads; it means the ads you see are less likely to be
based on your interests.

**Consent where it is required.** In the European Economic Area, the United
Kingdom and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalized ads
are requested, using Google's User Messaging Platform. You can change that
choice at any time from **Settings → Ad privacy options** inside the app. Where
local law does not require a consent form, none is shown.

## 4. Log Data

We do not collect Log Data. Because Patchwork Farm does not communicate with any
server of ours, we do not receive any diagnostic information, IP addresses, or
usage statistics from your use of the app.

Our advertising provider does receive technical information, including your IP
address, when an ad is requested — see Section 3. Crash and diagnostic reporting
outside of advertising, if any, is handled by the operating system or app store
according to their own privacy policies.

## 5. Service Providers

We use **Google AdMob** as a third-party service provider for advertising, as
described in Section 3. Google processes the information it collects under its
own privacy policy, linked in Section 2.

Aside from advertising, we do not employ any third-party companies to collect or
process your Personal Information on our behalf. The only other third parties
involved are the app stores (Google Play and the Apple App Store) that
distribute the app, which operate under their own privacy policies.

## 6. Security

We value your trust. Patchwork Farm keeps all of its own data locally on your
device and does not transmit it, so there is no transfer of your personal
information to us over the internet for us to secure. Information collected by
our advertising provider is transmitted and secured under Google's own
practices. We nevertheless encourage you to protect your device using the
security features it provides.

## 7. Links to Other Sites

Advertisements shown in Patchwork Farm may contain links to third-party websites
and app store listings that are not operated by us. If you tap an ad and are
taken to a third-party site, you are subject to that site's privacy policy. We
have no control over, and assume no responsibility for, the content or privacy
practices of any third-party sites, and we strongly advise you to review their
privacy policies.

## 8. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children
under 13, and the app itself collects none from anyone.

Patchwork Farm is a general-audience app and is not directed to children.
Advertising is configured to request a maximum ad content rating suitable for
general audiences. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has
provided information to us, please contact us so that we can address it.

## 9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to
review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes
by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page, and by updating the Effective
Date at the top. These changes are effective immediately after they are posted
on this page.
