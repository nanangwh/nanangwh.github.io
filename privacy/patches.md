# Privacy Policy for Patches

**Effective Date:** August 18, 2026

*Previous version: July 18, 2026. This update was made because Patches now shows advertisements — see Section 3.*

nnwh ("we," "us," or "our") built the Patches app as a free app. This SERVICE is provided by nnwh at no cost and is intended for use as is.

Patches is a calm tiling puzzle game. The game itself runs entirely on your device: your settings and best times never leave it, and **we do not collect, store, or share your personal information**. The app does show advertisements, and our advertising partner (Google AdMob) does collect some device information in order to serve them. Section 3 explains exactly what that involves.

This page is used to inform visitors regarding our policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of Personal Information if anyone decided to use our Service. If you choose to use our Service, then you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

## 1. Information Collection and Use
We do **not** collect any personally identifiable information through Patches. The app does not require an account, does not ask you to sign in, and does not transmit your game data to us or to any server of ours.

Patches stores a small amount of information **locally on your device only**, so the game can remember your preferences and progress:

- Your settings (such as night mode, show timer, gentle haptics, and gentle sounds);
- Your best completion times for each difficulty;
- Small counters recording how many puzzles you have solved and how many times the app has been opened, used to decide when to show the app store's native "rate this app" prompt and when an advertisement is due.

This information never leaves your device, is not accessible to us, and is permanently removed when you uninstall the app.

Separately from the above, our advertising provider collects device information directly from your device in order to serve ads. We never see that information in a form that identifies you. See Section 3.

## 2. Third-Party Services
Patches uses the following third-party services:

- **Google AdMob** — serves the advertisements described in Section 3. AdMob collects device information to select and measure ads.
- **App store review prompt** — the app may ask you to rate it using the native review feature provided by the Apple App Store or Google Play. This dialog is handled entirely by the platform; we do not receive your review or any personal data from it.
- **Fonts** — the app's typeface is bundled inside the app and loaded locally, with no network request.

## 3. Advertising
Patches displays advertisements supplied by **Google AdMob**.

**Where ads appear.** Ads are shown as an occasional full-screen ("interstitial") ad, and only at one point: after you have finished a puzzle and chosen to start another one. Ads never appear while you are solving a puzzle, never cover the board, and are deliberately infrequent — the app enforces a minimum gap between them, does not show any ad during your first session, and does not show any until you have completed several puzzles.

**What is collected.** To select and measure ads, Google AdMob may collect and process:

- Your device's **advertising identifier** (the Android Advertising ID, or on Apple devices the Identifier for Advertisers where you have permitted it);
- **Device and technical information**, such as device model, operating system version, language, coarse location derived from your IP address, and your IP address itself;
- **Ad interaction data**, such as which ads were shown, whether an ad was viewed or clicked, and crash or performance diagnostics relating to the ad.

This information is collected by Google directly from your device. We do not receive it, and we cannot link it to you. We receive only aggregate, anonymous reporting — for example, how many ads were shown in total.

**Your choices.** You can limit or reset ad personalization at any time using the controls built into your device:

- **Android:** Settings → Google → All services → Ads, where you can delete or reset your advertising ID and opt out of ad personalization.
- **iOS:** Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, where you can refuse app tracking; and Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising.

Opting out does not remove ads; it means the ads you see are less likely to be based on your interests.

**If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom**, you have rights over your personal data under the GDPR, including rights of access, erasure, and objection to processing for direct marketing. Because the advertising data described above is collected by Google rather than by us, requests relating to it are best directed to Google using the links in Section 2; you can also contact us at the address in Section 10 and we will help where we can. You can object to interest-based advertising at any time using the device controls above.

## 4. Log Data
We do not collect Log Data. Because Patches does not communicate with any server of ours, we do not receive any diagnostic information, IP addresses, or usage statistics from your use of the app.

Our advertising provider does receive technical information, including your IP address, when an ad is requested — see Section 3. Crash and diagnostic reporting outside of advertising, if any, is handled by the operating system or app store according to their own privacy policies.

## 5. Service Providers
We use **Google AdMob** as a third-party service provider for advertising, as described in Section 3. Google processes the information it collects under its own privacy policy, linked in Section 2.

Aside from advertising, we do not employ any third-party companies to collect or process your Personal Information on our behalf. The only other third parties involved are the app stores (Apple App Store and Google Play) that distribute the app, which operate under their own privacy policies.

## 6. Security
We value your trust. Patches keeps all of its own data locally on your device and does not transmit it, so there is no transfer of your personal information to us over the internet for us to secure. Information collected by our advertising provider is transmitted and secured under Google's own practices. We nevertheless encourage you to protect your device using the security features it provides.

## 7. Links to Other Sites
Advertisements shown in Patches may contain links to third-party websites and app store listings that are not operated by us. If you tap an ad and are taken to a third-party site, you are subject to that site's privacy policy. We have no control over, and assume no responsibility for, the content or privacy practices of any third-party sites, and we strongly advise you to review their privacy policies.

## 8. Children's Privacy
We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13, and the app itself collects none from anyone.

Patches is a general-audience app and is not directed to children. Advertising is configured to request a maximum ad content rating suitable for general audiences. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided information to us, please contact us so that we can address it.

## 9. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page, and by updating the Effective Date at the top. These changes are effective immediately after they are posted on this page.
