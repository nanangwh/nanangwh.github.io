# Privacy Policy for Frosty Slide

**Effective date:** 2 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **nnwh** ("we", "us", or "our") handles
information in connection with the mobile game **Frosty Slide** (the "App").
By downloading or using the App, you agree to the practices described in this
policy.

---

## 1. Summary

**Frosty Slide does not collect, store, or share any personal information.**
The App works fully offline. Your progress and settings are stored **only on
your own device** and are never sent to us or to any third party.

We do not require an account, we do not track you, and the App contains no
advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting software.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Data stored locally on your device

To remember your progress, the App saves the following **locally on your device
only**, using your device's standard app storage:

- Which levels you have **completed**, with your **best move count** and **best
  snowflake rating** for each level;
- The **area you last played** (Pine Lake, Frozen Falls, or Snowy Peak);
- Which **"joins the team" introduction cards** you have already seen;
- Your **settings** (sound effects, music, and haptics on/off).

This data never leaves your device through the App, is not transmitted to us
or to any server, and is not accessible to us in any way.

(If you have turned on your device's own backup, such as Android Auto Backup or
iCloud Backup, a copy of this data may be included in that backup under your own
account and the platform's terms. This is a function of your device, not of the
App.)

### 2.2 Information we do **not** collect

We do **not** collect, request, or have access to:

- Your name, email address, phone number, or any contact details;
- Your precise or approximate location;
- Your photos, contacts, files, camera, or microphone;
- Any advertising identifiers;
- Any usage analytics, crash reports, or behavioral data.

---

## 3. Device Permissions

The App does not request any special permissions. It does not use your
location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or files, and it does not need
an internet connection to play.

- **Haptics (vibration)** give tactile feedback during play. You can turn this
  off in Settings. It does not collect any data.

---

## 4. Links

The Settings screen includes a link to this Privacy Policy. Tapping it opens the
page in your web browser, which is governed by your browser's own privacy
practices. The App itself does not send any data when you do this.

---

## 5. Third-Party Services

The App does **not** include any third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking
software development kits (SDKs). The App is distributed through app stores
(such as Google Play and the Apple App Store), whose own privacy policies apply
to your use of those stores:

- Google: <https://policies.google.com/privacy>
- Apple: <https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/>

---

## 6. Children's Privacy

Frosty Slide is a family-friendly puzzle game suitable for all ages. We do
**not** knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including
children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in some jurisdictions). Because the
App collects no personal data at all, it does not create a privacy risk for
children. If you have any concerns, please contact us (see Section 10).

---

## 7. Data Retention and Deletion

Because all App data is stored only on your device, **you are in full control
of it**. You can delete it at any time by:

- **Clearing the App's data** in your device settings (on Android: Settings →
  Apps → Frosty Slide → Storage → Clear data), or
- **Uninstalling** the App, which removes all locally stored data.

We do not retain any of your data because we never receive it.

---

## 8. Data Security

The most effective protection for your data is that it never leaves your
device. Data stored on your device is protected by your device's own
operating-system security.

---

## 9. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under laws such as the GDPR
(European Economic Area, United Kingdom, and Switzerland) or the CCPA/CPRA
(California), including the right to access, correct, or delete your personal
data, and to opt out of its sale or sharing. Because we do not collect, hold,
sell, or share any personal data about you, there is nothing on our side to
provide, correct, delete, or opt out of. Your data lives only on your device and
can be removed as described in Section 7.
