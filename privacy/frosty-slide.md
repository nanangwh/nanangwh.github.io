# Privacy Policy for Frosty Slide

**Effective date:** 10 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **nnwh** ("we", "us", or "our") handles
information in connection with the mobile game **Frosty Slide** (the "App").
By downloading or using the App, you agree to the practices described in this
policy.

---

## 1. Summary

- Your **progress and settings stay on your device**. We never receive them.
- The App uses two Google services, **Firebase Analytics** and **Firebase
  Crashlytics**, to tell us how the game is played and when it crashes. They
  send **anonymous gameplay events and crash reports** to Google.
- We do **not** ask for an account, your name, your email address or any
  contact details, and we cannot tell who you are from the data we receive.
- The App contains **no advertising** and does **not** use your advertising ID.
- The game itself plays **fully offline**. Analytics and crash reports are sent
  only when your device is online.

---

## 2. Information Stored Only on Your Device

To remember your progress, the App saves the following **locally on your
device**, using your device's standard app storage:

- Which levels you have **completed**, with your **best move count** and **best
  snowflake rating** for each level;
- The **area you last played** (Pine Lake, Frozen Falls, or Snowy Peak);
- Which **"joins the team" introduction cards** you have already seen;
- Your **settings** (sound effects, music, and haptics on/off);
- Whether the App has already asked you to rate it.

The App does not send this save data to us or to any server.

(If you have turned on your device's own backup, such as Android Auto Backup, a
copy of this data may be included in that backup under your own account and the
platform's terms. This is a function of your device, not of the App.)

---

## 3. Information Collected Automatically

### 3.1 Gameplay analytics (Firebase Analytics)

The App uses Google's Firebase Analytics to understand how the game is played,
for example which levels are too hard. It collects:

- **Gameplay events:** a level being started, finished or left; the number of
  moves, hints and restarts used and the snowflakes earned; a new set of levels
  being opened; your answer to the "Down to one snowflake" card; a suggestion
  to try another team being shown or taken; a request to rate the App being
  made; and which screen is shown.
- **An app-instance identifier:** a random ID created when the App is
  installed. It identifies the installation, not you, and is reset if you
  clear the App's data or reinstall it.
- **Device and app information:** device model, operating-system version,
  language, screen size, and the App's version.
- **Approximate location:** your country or region and city, which Google
  derives from your IP address. The IP address itself is not stored, and the
  App never uses your device's precise location.

### 3.2 Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

When the App crashes or hits an error, Firebase Crashlytics sends a report so
we can fix it. A report contains:

- The **error and the code path** that led to it (a stack trace);
- The **level** being played at the time;
- **Device state** when it happened: device model, operating-system version,
  orientation, free memory and storage, and the App's version;
- A random **installation identifier** used to count how many installations a
  crash affects.

### 3.3 What we do not collect

We do **not** collect, request, or have access to:

- Your name, email address, phone number, or any contact details;
- Your precise location;
- Your photos, contacts, files, camera, or microphone;
- Your **advertising ID**: the App removes the advertising-ID permission and
  turns off advertising-ID collection in Firebase Analytics.

---

## 4. How We Use This Information

We use analytics and crash data only to:

- Find and fix crashes and errors;
- Tune the game, for example its difficulty and the order of levels;
- Understand, in total, how many people play and how far they get.

We do **not** use it for advertising, we do **not** build profiles of
individual players, and we do **not** sell it.

---

## 5. Who Receives This Information

Analytics and crash data is sent to and processed by **Google** (Google LLC and
Google Ireland Limited), which provides Firebase to us as a service provider
(a "data processor"). We do not share it with anyone else. Google may process
it on servers outside your country.

- How Google uses data from apps that use its services:
  <https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites>
- Firebase privacy and security: <https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy>
- Google's privacy policy: <https://policies.google.com/privacy>

All of it is sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection.

The App is distributed through Google Play, whose own privacy policy applies to
your use of the store. When the App asks you to rate it, the rating card is
shown and handled by Google Play.

---

## 6. Device Permissions

The App requests only ordinary, automatically granted Android permissions, and
only so that Firebase can send analytics and crash reports:

- **Internet access** and **network state**, to send the data described in
  Section 3 when the device is online;
- **Wake lock**, so a report that has started sending can finish.

The App does not use your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or
files, and it does not need an internet connection to play.

- **Haptics (vibration)** give tactile feedback during play. You can turn this
  off in Settings. It does not collect any data.

## 8. Children's Privacy

Frosty Slide is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not
knowingly collect personal information from them. The App never asks for a
name, an email address, or any other contact details. If you believe a child
has provided personal information through the App, please contact us (see
Section 12).

---

## 9. Data Retention and Deletion

**On your device.** You are in full control of your save data. You can delete
it at any time by:

- **Clearing the App's data** in your device settings (on Android: Settings →
  Apps → Frosty Slide → Storage → Clear data), or
- **Uninstalling** the App.

Either one also resets the random identifiers described in Section 3, so later
data is no longer linked to earlier data.

**Held by Google for us.** Analytics data tied to an app-instance identifier is
kept for up to 14 months, and crash reports for 90 days, after which Google
deletes them. Totals that cannot be linked to an installation may be kept
longer.

Because the App has no accounts, we cannot look up one person's data by name or
email address. If you would like your analytics data deleted, contact us (see
Section 12) and we will tell you how to find your app-instance identifier so
that we can ask Google to delete the data linked to it.

---

## 10. Data Security

Your save data is protected by your device's own operating-system security.
Analytics and crash data is encrypted in transit and stored by Google under its
security practices. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure,
but we collect as little as we can.

## 11. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under laws such as the GDPR
(European Economic Area, United Kingdom, and Switzerland) or the CCPA/CPRA
(California), including the right to access, correct, or delete your personal
data, to object to its processing, and to opt out of its sale or sharing.

We do not sell or share personal data. Where the GDPR applies, we process the
analytics and crash data described above on the basis of our legitimate
interest in keeping the App working and improving it. To exercise any of your
rights, contact us (see Section 12).
